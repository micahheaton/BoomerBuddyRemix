# Customer Success

## Definition of customer value

A household is healthy when the protected person can independently check something suspicious, understands the result, has a safe next action, and has an appropriate escalation path. Payment or login frequency alone is not health.

## Explainable health signals

- orientation/practice state;
- mobile or web access established;
- consensual Trusted Circle relationship;
- successful first check and return use;
- unresolved high-risk incident or support case;
- repeated inconclusive results;
- inactivity relative to the member’s own pattern;
- payment failure, cancellation intent, or revoked household consent.

Show contributing factors rather than an opaque “AI health score.” Never infer cognitive decline or vulnerability from behavior.

## Lifecycle workflows

Automate respectful reminders for incomplete orientation, invite expiry, unresolved incidents, and payment recovery. Suppress promotional messages during incidents. Cancellation must be easy, preserve access through the paid period, explain data export/deletion, and avoid fear-based save copy. Advocacy/referral prompts occur only after a completed value event and remain dismissible.

## Build Run 1

HQ shows seeded orientation and household state plus a deterministic `needs_attention` reason. Real outbound email/SMS, save offers, win-back, and staffed case management are deferred pending consent, vendor, training, and unit-economic decisions.
