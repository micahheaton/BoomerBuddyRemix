# Founder Decisions

Implementation can continue under the recommended defaults below; decisions become binding before the stated milestone.

## 1. Brand commitment

- **Recommendation:** retain BoomerBuddy as a working brand; delay major spend until interviews and preliminary trademark/domain counsel.
- **Alternatives:** dual-brand enterprise offer; rename.
- **Tradeoff:** memorable warmth versus possible age-label resistance and lower enterprise gravity.
- **Founder decision by:** brand identity purchase or public launch.

## 2. Launch packaging and price

- **Recommendation:** useful Free check plus Family hero hypothesis at $14.99/month or $149/year; test $119/year only as a controlled founding offer and test an $8.99 individual plan without making it the hero.
- **Alternatives:** Family-only paid; annual-first; partner-only.
- **Tradeoff:** learning speed, conversion, margin, and packaging complexity.
- **Founder decision by:** paid landing-page test; no real charges before evidence/legal gates.

## 3. Initial go-to-market sequence

- **Recommendation:** validate direct family demand while conducting narrow credit-union discovery; do not wait for enterprise revenue.
- **Alternatives:** B2C-only or partner-first.
- **Tradeoff:** fast learning versus partner leverage and sales-cycle distraction.
- **Founder decision by:** first material growth spend.

## 4. Human Safety Setup

- **Recommendation:** keep it as a research concept, not a launch promise.
- **Alternatives:** paid concierge pilot or included Family onboarding.
- **Tradeoff:** trust/activation leverage versus staffing, training, liability, and margin.
- **Founder decision by:** after self-serve orientation research.

## 5. Aggressive investment envelope

- **Recommendation:** prioritize real user research, fraud evaluation data/adjudication, security/privacy review, accessibility testing, and one strong intelligence source before broad acquisition.
- **Alternatives:** brand/paid growth or feature engineering first.
- **Tradeoff:** slower visible growth versus lower existential safety and product risk.
- **Founder decision by:** first external budget allocation.
