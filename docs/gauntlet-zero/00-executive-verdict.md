# Executive Verdict

## Conditional company go; Build Run 1 gate passed

BoomerBuddy is worth pursuing as a **consented household scam-response service**, not as a generic AI detector. The need is real: the FTC reports $2.4 billion in fraud losses reported by adults 60+ for 2024 and stresses substantial underreporting uncertainty. The commercial opportunity is plausible because fear, verification friction, and family coordination are persistent problems. Paid demand, brand response, retention, and measurable prevention value remain unproven.

## What the company sells

The scanner is acquisition and habit formation. The paid product reduces time from suspicion to a safer action and, with explicit consent, connects a protected person to trusted help. Trend Micro already combines multi-artifact checking with a six-person Family Circle, so scanner plus family sharing is not differentiation. Durable value must come from paired orientation, private-by-default permissions, evidence-separated reasoning, verified action and recovery follow-through, accountable quality evidence, and trusted distribution. That position is still a hypothesis.

## Initial focus

- **Primary payer:** adult child or household organizer.
- **Primary protected user:** an older adult who willingly uses the product.
- **Hero SKU hypothesis:** useful Free check plus Family at $14.99/month or $149/year; test $119/year only as a controlled founding offer and validate before charging.
- **Core input:** text and URL first; native share workflows next.
- **Distribution:** direct family validation plus narrow credit-union discovery; no custom enterprise build before product proof.

## Why this could fail

1. A false negative or unsafe action causes harm and destroys trust.
2. Family permissions drift into surveillance or coercion.
3. Customers will use free AI instead of paying for the surrounding workflow.
4. The BoomerBuddy name repels part of the user or sponsor audience.
5. Long partner sales cycles exhaust a lean company before repeatable activation.

## Investment posture

Spend aggressively first on paired user research, an independently adjudicated fraud/action evaluation corpus, security/privacy review, older-adult accessibility testing, and one measured intelligence source. Defer broad paid acquisition, a large sales team, concierge promises, and overlapping enterprise vendors.

## Gate decision

The independent Gauntlet Zero review passed on 2026-08-15 for the bounded, local, synthetic-data Build Run 1 in [45-readiness-gate.md](./45-readiness-gate.md). The build uses local providers and reversible hypotheses, so it requires no production credentials, customer data, payment, deployment, URL fetch, or external action. This is not a customer-launch or first-dollar approval.
