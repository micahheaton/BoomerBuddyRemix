Status: **designed; no hiring or vendor engagement**

## Finding

The staged staffing plan is workload-driven. Subscriber count is context, never a requisition. The canonical [Staffing and Philippines Operations](./STAFFING-AND-PHILIPPINES-OPS.md) document defines role boundaries, triggers, cost sensitivities, security controls, and BPO/direct-employment gates.

Run 2 implements a 120-productive-hour monthly trigger for five capacities. That equates to 360 support cases at 20 minutes each, 240 assisted orientations at 30 minutes, 288 bounded fraud reviews at 25 minutes, 288 customer-success interventions at 25 minutes, or 206 partner accounts with 35 minutes of due-action work. Start fractional help only after 60 hours persists for two months; preserve a 20% buffer and named backup before publishing a service level.

## Staging judgment

- At first dollar, named security/privacy/legal/fraud/accessibility/accounting accountability is required regardless of volume; these can be fractional professionals.
- At 1K, test fractional L1 capacity only if measured demand fires the early gate.
- At 5K–10K, a dedicated support or review role exists only if its own queue exceeds 120 hours; do not combine L1, Trust & Safety, and customer success into an unsafe generalist role.
- At 25K, shifts and QA follow arrival curves and severity, not a promise of 24×7 support.
- At 50K, 10–20 operations FTE-equivalents plus 5–8 senior/product/platform/revenue roles is a sensitivity range, not a hiring plan. Payroll must remain inside the [$2.1M base envelope](./50K-SUBSCRIBER-MODEL.md) or the `$1M` profit scenario fails.

## Philippines adjudication

A future Philippines team is reasonable for approved FAQs, navigation, orientation assistance, billing self-service, content-free triage, and tightly rubriced redacted review. L1 must not see raw scam artifacts by default, pronounce something safe, reset identity without proof, browse households, make material refunds, or provide novel fraud/legal advice. Difficult adjudication remains with supervised senior Trust & Safety expertise.

The Philippines Data Privacy Act leaves BoomerBuddy accountable for outsourced data and requires safeguards; its rules require processor contracts, documented instructions, confidentiality, security, and comparable transfer protection. DOLE rules prohibit labor-only contracting and require compliant providers. These are counsel and diligence gates, not a reason to label a vendor “compliant.” Sources: [National Privacy Commission](https://privacy.gov.ph/implementing-rules-regulations-data-privacy-act-2012/) and [DOLE Department Order 174](https://dole.gov.ph/news/bello-signs-d-o-on-contracting-and-subcontracting/).

Government wage data establish a floor/context, not a hiring quote: PSA reports `₱29,310` average monthly wage in NCR for covered full-time formal workers in August 2024, while the current NCR non-agriculture minimum is `₱755/day`. Skilled, secure operations should budget above those anchors and include 13th-month pay, statutory employer contributions, equipment, supervision, QA, shift cost, and vendor margin. The canonical model uses wide direct-loaded and BPO bands and requires current comparable quotes.
