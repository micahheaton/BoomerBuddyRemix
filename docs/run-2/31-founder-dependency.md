# 31 — Founder Dependency

Status: **governance implemented; operating reduction unproven**

## Finding

The canonical [Founder Dependency Model](./FOUNDER-DEPENDENCY-MODEL.md) defines a reproducible ratio: target founder minutes divided by the same workflow basket’s current founder minutes. It protects high-value founder activity instead of treating every founder hour as waste.

A reference operating month contains 205.5 modeled founder hours. It retains 42 hours of executive review, major product decisions, original fraud/security content, major partner/media work, and material incident leadership. Routine support, billing, customer success, lead research, reporting, fraud review, task assignment, bookkeeping preparation, monitoring, and content repurposing fall from 163.5 to 7.18 founder hours through bounded automation and delegation. The modeled score moves from **100 to 24**, leaving 49.18 founder hours/month. These are assumptions, not a time study or achieved result.

## Implemented control plane

Run 2 implements versioned autonomy policies, allowed data/tools, per-action budgets, approval/run records, audit, Owner Attention, and Owner Brief persistence. The global automation kill switch defaults engaged. Code allows only reversible internal `AUTO` actions such as briefs, summaries, drafts, deterministic scoring, stale-work identification, internal tasks, provider health, approved maintenance, and content-free attribution. An operator policy may narrow but not expand that boundary.

This does not mean those workflows operate autonomously in production. No production scheduler, staff, professional integration, outbound messaging account, or live queue exists. Current live-operation dependency remains **100 by policy** until external execution and delegation are proved.

## Preserve versus remove

Preserve founder public voice, authoritative original content, strategic partnerships, capital allocation, material product direction, weekly executive review, and exceptional escalation. Progressively remove routine support, reconciliation, basic success, lead-list work, repetitive follow-up/reporting, routine fraud review, work assignment, bookkeeping preparation, monitoring, and repurposing.

Automation only creates internal reversible work. Consumer/prospect contact, publication, prices, material refunds, permissions, hiring, contracts, and novel safety advice require approval or human judgment. Legal, privacy, accounting, tax, independent security, accessibility, and employment classification remain professional responsibilities.

## Operating gate

Run a four-week founder time study before claiming improvement. Freeze the workflow denominator for a quarter, reconcile it to actual queues, and report total founder hours beside high-value hours. Each recurrence needs a named owner, backup, runbook, service level, and escalation. Re-engage the kill switch on data/tool drift, incorrect action, budget/audit failure, or incident.

The scaled target is no more than **24** on the reference basket, not “zero founder.” A two-week founder-vacation drill should show that routine work continues while consequential decisions wait safely or reach a named executive backup. No target authorizes unsafe autonomy, concealed offshore labor, ignored queues, or launch.
