# People, Hiring, and Workforce

## Build/buy boundary

HQ should know who may perform BoomerBuddy work, their role, team, queue, training state, and system-access lifecycle. Payroll, benefits, tax, time tracking, background checks, applicant tracking, and performance management belong in specialist systems.

## Early roles

Founder/owner, support specialist, fraud analyst, customer success, partnerships/revenue, and system administrator. Separate customer-support and privileged system administration; no universal “admin” role. Contractors receive expiring, least-privilege access.

## Required controls

- joiner/mover/leaver checklist tied to identity and role changes;
- MFA and managed-device expectations for privileged roles;
- queue assignment instead of broad database browsing;
- training acknowledgment before sensitive work;
- step-up and just-in-time grants for exceptional customer-content access;
- immutable access/audit events and periodic access review;
- prompt offboarding and preservation of business records.

## Recruiting decision

Do not build an ATS. A public `/careers` page and an integration reference are sufficient until recurring hiring volume creates a real workflow problem. Build Run 1 includes only seeded employee roles needed to prove HQ authorization.

Employee quality metrics should measure case outcomes and review quality, not invasive surveillance or keystroke/activity volume.
