# Personas and Jobs to Be Done

Status: **hypotheses requiring interviews and usability testing**.

## Primary commercial pairing

The primary early payer is an **adult child or household organizer** worried about an older relative. The primary protected user is the **older adult** who must willingly use the check flow. Self-purchasing older adults and couples remain important segments, but marketing and UX must not assume payer and user are the same person.

| Role | Core job | Required trust condition | Early success signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Protected older adult | “Help me decide what to do without making me feel foolish.” | Control over what is shared | Completes a practice check and returns independently |
| Adult child / household owner | “Help my family intervene before panic becomes loss.” | Visibility is consent-based, not surveillance | Invites and activates a protected member |
| Couple | “Help us verify suspicious requests together.” | Symmetric permissions | Both can check and escalate |
| Trusted Circle member | “Give me enough evidence to help quickly.” | Least-privilege, purpose-limited access | Responds to an escalation safely |
| Caregiver | “Support an authorized person without overstepping.” | Relationship and authority are explicit | Uses only granted permissions |
| Adviser | “Help my client verify through official channels.” | No implied fiduciary or bank authority | Provides an approved verification route |
| Sponsor / partner | “Protect members and demonstrate adoption safely.” | Tenant isolation and privacy-preserving reporting | Eligible households activate and retain |
| Employee | “Resolve the right case without browsing customer data.” | Queue-based, audited, just-in-time access | Meets quality/SLA without excess access |

## Critical distinctions

`Person`, `identity`, `payer`, `household owner`, `protected member`, `Trusted Circle member`, `organization member`, and `employee` are separate domain concepts. One person may hold several roles, but no role is inferred from age, payment, family relationship, or organization membership.

## Validation plan

Before paid launch, conduct at least 12 moderated sessions split across older adults, adult children, and pairs. Test: name reaction, first check, uncertainty comprehension, invitation consent, escalation expectations, and willingness-to-pay. Include participants with low vision, reduced dexterity, low digital confidence, and screen-reader use. Record observed behavior and exact objections; do not treat stated enthusiasm as activation evidence.
