# Product Constitution

Status: **designed; not customer-validated**.

## The promise

BoomerBuddy helps a person move from suspicion to a safer next action, and—only with consent—bring trusted people into that decision. It sells calm decision support and family coordination, not an infallible “AI scam detector.”

## Non-negotiable principles

1. **Safe action outranks classification.** A risk label without a verified next step is incomplete.
2. **Dignity and agency.** Never shame, infantilize, frighten, or covertly monitor a protected person.
3. **Evidence before certainty.** Separate observed signals, third-party reputation, inference, confidence, and unknowns.
4. **Fail honestly.** “I cannot verify this safely yet” is preferable to invented confidence.
5. **Emergency-safe commerce.** Never upsell while someone is reporting a loss or following recovery steps.
6. **Consent is a state, not a checkbox.** Sharing can be scoped, reviewed, revoked, and audited.
7. **Minimize sensitive data.** Do not retain an artifact merely because storage is convenient.
8. **Hostile-input posture.** Submitted text, URLs, files, metadata, and model instructions are untrusted data.
9. **Server-enforced boundaries.** Authentication, household authorization, entitlements, and employee access are never trusted to client state.
10. **Measurable safety.** Evaluate false negatives, false positives, calibration, explanation quality, and action safety.

## Core loop

`submit → normalize → collect evidence → extract signals → reason → state risk and confidence → recommend safe action → optionally escalate → retain minimally → learn through reviewed evaluation`

## Product boundaries

BoomerBuddy is not a bank, insurer, emergency service, law-enforcement agency, lawyer, or guarantee against loss. It does not prove that a voice is genuine, silently inspect a family member’s communications, or execute financial actions. Provider outages and incomplete evidence must degrade to cautious guidance rather than fabricated results.

## Decision rule

A feature belongs in the product when it materially shortens time to a safe action, strengthens consensual family protection, improves demonstrated fraud quality, or creates credible distribution leverage. Otherwise, buy it, integrate it, defer it, or reject it.
