## Evidence classes and controls

- **Confirmed:** directly supported by the linked primary source or cited local repository path.
- **Inference:** a reasoned conclusion from confirmed facts; label it and preserve contrary evidence.
- **Hypothesis:** requires user, technical, safety, or commercial validation.
- **Decision:** an explicit chosen direction with alternatives and consequences, normally in an ADR or founder register.

Every material claim records owner, source, scope, access/review date, and expiry/recheck trigger. A vendor page supports only what the vendor currently publishes, not independent quality or outcomes. Search results are discovery aids, not evidence. If a cited source changes or disappears, retain a lawful snapshot/reference where permitted, mark the claim stale, and stop using it until reviewed.
