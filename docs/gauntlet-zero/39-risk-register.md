# Risk Register

| Rank | Risk | Likelihood / impact | Early indicator | Mitigation / stop condition |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | False negative contributes to financial loss | Medium / critical | malicious golden cases missed; unsafe action advice | conservative uncertainty, multi-signal pipeline, action evaluation, disclaimers, incident review; stop launch on critical regression |
| 2 | Family feature becomes surveillance or abuse | Medium / critical | permission complaints, coerced invites, unexplained access | explicit consent, private-by-default artifacts, granular grants, revocation, step-up, audit |
| 3 | Paid demand is weaker than founder enthusiasm | High / high | low practice-to-paid conversion; interview objections | validate buyer/problem/name/pricing before large build or acquisition spend |
| 4 | Commodity free AI erodes scanner value | High / high | users value only one-off classification | sell workflow, verified action, family coordination, recovery, intelligence and distribution |
| 5 | Sensitive artifacts leak through app/vendor/employee access | Medium / critical | overbroad logs, retention drift, unusual access | minimization, field encryption, no content analytics, JIT employee access, vendor review, incident plan |
| 6 | Reputation/LLM provider creates false authority or lock-in | Medium / high | one provider dominates verdicts/cost | provider ports, source provenance, deterministic baseline, evaluation before change |
| 7 | App-store or subscription policy disrupts packaging | Medium / high | rejection or entitlement mismatch | canonical entitlements, current policy review, platform adapters, reconciliation |
| 8 | B2B2C sales cycle consumes company before proof | Medium / high | custom requests without activated users | narrow pilot criteria, paid/limited pilots, reusable integration boundary |
| 9 | Brand alienates protected users or lacks enterprise gravity | Medium / medium | negative interview language; sponsor hesitation | keep as working name, test with both buyer/user, trademark counsel before spend |
| 10 | Founder/operator overload | High / high | aging queues and unresolved incidents | narrow launch, automation with review, explicit service levels, hire by queue trigger |

Owners and due dates are assigned when the project has named people beyond the founder. Risks 1, 2, and 5 block first-dollar launch if controls or evidence are inadequate.
