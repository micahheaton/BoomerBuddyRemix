# B2B and B2B2C Strategy

Status: **prioritization hypothesis; no buyer, pilot or partnership is secured**. Research accessed 2026-08-15.

## Commercial thesis

Institutions may sponsor BoomerBuddy to deepen trust, support families and provide a safer escalation path. They will not buy an “AI scam detector” merely because fraud losses are large. The product must show member/employee adoption, safe outcomes, low complaint burden, security readiness and a benefit the buyer cannot deliver more cheaply through education or an incumbent.

The preferred initial motion is **sponsored B2B2C with a low-integration, co-branded eligibility experience**. Avoid core financial data, automated transaction intervention and bespoke APIs until product value and controls are proven.

## Segment ranking

| Segment | User / economic buyer | Why it may buy | Friction | Priority hypothesis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Credit unions | Members and families / digital, fraud, member-experience executive | Trust, member education, family protection | Vendor diligence, budget, proof; Carefull already sells here | 1 |
| RIAs / broker-dealers | Clients/families / compliance, client service, practice leader | Trusted-contact and family-risk conversation | Regulatory review, fragmented firms, adviser workflow | 2 |
| Employer caregiver benefits | Employees caring for adults / benefits leader or broker | Caregiver burden and work disruption | Benefit overload, annual cycles, engagement | 3 |
| Caregiver/senior media and affinity groups | Audience / publisher, association or subscriber-growth lead | Useful timely content and member value | Endorsement trust, affiliate economics | 3 as acquisition; later as sponsor |
| Insurers | Policyholders / innovation, prevention, claims or benefits | Potential risk mitigation and engagement | Long proof cycle; loss attribution; procurement | 4 |
| Senior living / home care | Residents/clients/families / operator | Family trust and staff escalation | Staff workflow, consent, decentralized budgets | 4 |
| Telecom, identity/security, OEM | Subscribers / product or partnership executive | Feature bundle and distribution | Build-versus-buy, deep integration, data/liability | 5 |
| Libraries/community organizations | Patrons / program lead, grant sponsor | Access and education | Limited direct budget, support load | Learning/distribution, not core revenue |

Rankings are strategic hypotheses, not market survey results. Retirement providers, banks, elder-law and estate-planning practices should remain in discovery but not receive simultaneous go-to-market investment.

Official context supports discovery, not demand. The 2025 AARP/NAC study estimates 63 million U.S. family caregivers ([AARP](https://www.aarp.org/press/releases/2025-07-24-new-report-reveals-crisis-point-for-americas-63-million-family-caregivers.html)); [FINRA guidance](https://www.finra.org/investors/insights/senior-financial-exploitation) describes broker-dealer trusted-contact practices; and the [SEC adviser statistics](https://www.sec.gov/data-research/statistics-data-visualizations/investment-adviser-statistics) describe a large registered-adviser sector. None identifies BoomerBuddy budget, intent or an addressable customer count.

## Buyer discovery

For each segment identify the end user, champion, economic buyer, security/privacy approver, legal/compliance approver, procurement owner and implementation lead. Ask:

- What happens today when a member or employee asks about a suspicious request?
- Which metric, budget and executive priority does this affect?
- What is already bought, built or offered free?
- What evidence is required to make, renew and expand a purchase?
- Which data or integration is unacceptable?
- What annual calendar and contracting steps control timing?

Record exact language and disconfirming evidence. A friendly conversation, logo permission request, unpaid “pilot” or letter of intent is not revenue validation.

## Offer architecture

Start with a 90–120 day **paid evaluation**: defined eligible cohort, co-branded enrollment, orientation, free check, consented household tools, monthly aggregate reporting, support and an agreed evaluation protocol. It must state that model results do not guarantee prevention and that the institution does not receive individual artifacts or risk details without explicit purpose-specific consent.

Commercial structures to test—not researched willingness to pay—are fixed paid pilot, per activated household plus minimum, or per eligible population. See [15-commercial-model-pricing.md](15-commercial-model-pricing.md). Scope security review and implementation separately; do not hide bespoke work in per-member price.

## Lifecycle and proof

Track:

**named account → qualified pain → champion → economic case → security/legal review → paid evaluation → launch-ready eligible cohort → enrolled → activated household → verified outcome → renewal → expansion**

At account level report stage age, next evidence, procurement blockers and forecast confidence. At cohort level report eligible-to-enrolled, orientation, first/second check, safe-action completion, Trusted Circle consent, support burden, complaints and retention. “Loss prevented” requires an independently defensible counterfactual and cannot be inferred from a warning.

## Validation plan and gates

1. Complete 30 discovery calls: at least 10 credit unions, five advice firms, five benefits buyers/brokers and ten across other segments.
2. Advance only segments where at least three independent buyers identify a budget owner, current workaround, purchase trigger and acceptable low-integration entry.
3. Obtain two paid design partners in one segment before building segment-specific integrations. Signed contracts must define data rights, security duties, claims, references and assignment.
4. Pre-register pilot metrics and renewal criteria with each buyer. Include a comparison or baseline where ethical and feasible.
5. Concentrate resources only after one evaluation renews or expands and cohort economics clear the gates in [36-unit-economics.md](36-unit-economics.md).

Stop a segment if buyers require unproven loss guarantees, regulated actions outside product scope, sensitive data before value, indefinite free pilots or custom work that cannot be reused.
