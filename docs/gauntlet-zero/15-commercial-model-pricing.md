# Commercial Model and Pricing

Status: **packaging and prices are hypotheses; no willingness-to-pay evidence exists**. Prices and policies checked 2026-08-15.

## What is being sold

The paid product is recurring household coordination and safer follow-through—not access to an AI classifier. Free use must resolve a real urgent question because free general AI, [Bitdefender Scamio](https://www.bitdefender.com/en-au/consumer/scamio), platform call screening and [AARP Fraud Watch](https://www.aarp.org/money/scams-fraud/about-fraud-watch-network/) constrain the price of a bare check.

Three payer motions require separate offers and economics:

1. **Consumer:** an adult child, caregiver or protected adult pays.
2. **Sponsored B2B2C:** an institution pays for eligible or activated households.
3. **Channel/reseller:** a partner sells or bundles the service and retains a negotiated share.

Do not merge them into one conversion rate or price.

## Consumer packaging hypotheses

| Plan | Working hypothesis | Boundary |
|---|---:|---|
| Free | $0; about five routine checks/month | Complete result, safe next steps and urgent recovery guidance; limited history and collaboration. |
| Plus | $8.99/month or $89/year | One protected adult, two Trusted Circle participants, history and follow-through. |
| Family | $14.99/month or $149/year | Up to three protected adults and six Trusted Circle participants, shared case coordination and family controls. |

These limits are experiment inputs, not commitments. Never describe resource-intensive use as “unlimited”; publish fair-use limits. Do not paywall immediate steps after suspected loss. A $119 Family annual offer may be tested as a time-limited founding price, but at roughly $9.92/month before fees it leaves much less room for app-store fees and human support than $149.

## Price context, not proof

[EverSafe](https://www.eversafe.com/pricing) publishes $7.49–$26.99 monthly tiers, [Aura](https://www.aura.com/pricing) lists $12/month annual for an individual and $32/month annual for a family, and [Carefull](https://getcarefull.com/) lists $29.99/month. Their bundles, brands, insurance and monitoring differ. They bound plausible category spend but do not prove BoomerBuddy’s willingness to pay. The v1 repository’s $4.95/month “unlimited” Premium offer is an unvalidated anchor, not evidence.

## Sponsored pricing hypotheses

Test all three structures in buyer interviews; present them as alternatives, not “market rates.”

- **Activated household:** $3–$6 per activated household per month plus an annual minimum. Aligns spend to adoption but invites billing disputes.
- **Eligible population:** $0.15–$0.40 per eligible member/employee per month. Predictable, but sponsors may resist paying before activation.
- **Fixed paid pilot:** $15,000–$30,000 for a defined cohort and term, creditable toward year one. Funds service and evaluation without implying a long-term unit price.

Any quote must specify implementation, support, minimums, data scope, renewal, usage, accessibility, security work and channel revenue share. No free bespoke integration.

## Validation sequence

1. Conduct problem and purchase-process interviews with 15 older adults, 15 adult children and 12 institutional buyers. Ask about recent behavior, budget owner and alternatives before showing price.
2. Run sequential-monadic concept testing with 200–300 qualified respondents. Randomize packages and prices; measure comprehension and purchase intent, recognizing that survey intent overstates purchase.
3. Use a transparent price page or waitlist test. State that the offer is a beta and do not charge, reserve a card or create false scarcity without explicit consent.
4. Enroll at least 50 households in a paid 90-day beta. Compare $8.99 Plus and $14.99 Family on activation, refunds, support minutes, repeat use and retention—not signup alone.
5. Reprice only after observed service cost and cohort behavior. Record the decision and grandfathering policy.

## Commerce guardrails

[Stripe](https://stripe.com/pricing) currently lists 2.9% + $0.30 for standard U.S. online cards. Mobile-store terms can materially change net revenue: Apple’s eligible [Small Business Program](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/small-business-program/) uses a 15% commission, while Google’s [service-fee policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/112622?hl=en) varies by program and transaction. Recheck before launch.

Show renewal price and period adjacent to the purchase action, obtain express consent, issue receipts and provide cancellation through the same surface without retention tricks. The FTC has specifically challenged hidden terms and difficult cancellation as dark patterns ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2021/10/ftc-ramp-enforcement-against-illegal-dark-patterns-trick-trap-consumers-subscriptions)). Counsel must review current federal and state requirements before sale.
