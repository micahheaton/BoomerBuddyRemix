# Strategic Value Register

## Assets that can compound

| Asset | Why a buyer may prefer acquisition | Day-one proof mechanism | Compounds? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recurring family revenue and retention | Demonstrates paid, durable demand | reconciled cohorts, plan/channel/churn definitions | Yes |
| Activated protected households | Relationship graph is harder than app code | consented household/Trusted Circle activation metrics | Yes |
| Fraud/action evaluation corpus | Improves quality and de-risks model/provider changes | versioned, licensed, human-reviewed golden sets and runs | Yes |
| Safety outcome operations | Repeatable adjudication and recovery quality | review queues, QA, incident learnings, action-safety metrics | Yes |
| Campaign/taxonomy knowledge | Current structured fraud intelligence | provenance, time bounds, reviewer history | Yes |
| Distribution agreements | Efficient access to high-trust audiences | contracts, eligible/enrolled/activated/retained partner cohorts | Yes |
| Consumer trust and brand | Lowers adoption friction in a fear-sensitive category | unaided awareness, trust research, organic/direct demand | Yes |
| Partner-ready entitlement platform | Makes sponsorship/OEM deployment practical | source-neutral plans, eligibility, isolation, reconciliation | Moderately |
| Consent/data lineage and security posture | Reduces diligence and regulatory risk | audit, DPAs, access reviews, deletion lineage, incident history | Yes |
| Software/IP | Accelerates integration but is reproducible alone | clean ownership, SBOM/licenses, tests, ADRs, release history | Slowly |

## Non-assets and traps

Raw sensitive submissions without clear consent/data rights are a liability, not a moat. Unvalidated AI prompts, vanity customer counts, one-off partner customizations, fabricated “loss prevented,” and dependence on a single model/vendor reduce value.

## Diligence practices to start now

Clean source/IP ownership; dependency licensing/SBOM; documented architecture decisions; reproducible builds/tests; metric definitions; provider and consent provenance; security/audit history; customer and partner contract indexing; revenue reconciliation; retention/deletion enforcement; evaluation versioning; and an honest incident register.

## Acquisition thesis

Likely strategic categories include identity/cybersecurity, telecommunications, financial institutions, insurers, caregiver/senior platforms, and consumer trust vendors. The thesis remains a hypothesis: build an independent business whose data rights, quality evidence, relationships, and recurring economics are useful across categories rather than tailoring the company to one imagined acquirer.
