# Operating Model

## Lean launch organization

The founder owns product, capital allocation, and partnerships. Engineering owns platform reliability and secure delivery. Fraud quality is a named operating function even before a dedicated hire: evaluation fixtures, regressions, incident review, provider changes, and campaign knowledge require an accountable cadence. Customer support starts as a constrained founder workflow, not an advertised 24/7 service.

## Core cadences

- **Daily:** failed jobs/providers, high-severity security alerts, open customer incidents.
- **Weekly:** fraud evaluation, false reports, activation, support backlog, stale pipeline, spend anomalies.
- **Monthly:** cohorts/churn, unit economics, access review, provider scorecard, privacy/deletion queue, accounting reconciliation.
- **Quarterly:** threat model, disaster-recovery exercise, vendor review, pricing evidence, partner outcomes, roadmap and risk register.

## Control principles

One accountable owner per queue; explicit severity and SLA; human approval for external or consequential actions; immutable audit for security/commerce/consent; separation of production and development; documented incident commander; post-incident learning without blame.

## Scale triggers

Hire or buy capability when queue load, response risk, or specialization—not vanity headcount—crosses a documented threshold. Security review, older-adult usability research, legal/privacy counsel, and fraud evaluation are earlier investments than a large sales or feature team.
