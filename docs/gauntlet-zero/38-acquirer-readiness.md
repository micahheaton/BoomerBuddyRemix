# Acquirer Readiness

Status: **no current basis for an acquisition valuation or buyer-interest claim**. The application alone is readily buildable; a strategic asset must be earned.

## Strategic thesis under challenge

A buyer would not need BoomerBuddy to obtain an LLM, URL lookup, senior-friendly screen or basic family invitation. Trend Micro already offers a scam checker with a multi-person Family Circle ([ScamCheck](https://trendlife.com/en-us/products/scam-check)); platforms and broad security suites bundle adjacent protection. “First AI scam app for seniors” is neither substantiated nor durable.

A credible buy-versus-build case would combine:

1. recurring, retained household and sponsor revenue;
2. repeatable institutional distribution with assignable contracts;
3. a consented family coordination workflow users actually activate;
4. rights-cleared, representative incident/evaluation data and calibrated safety quality;
5. verified safe-action/recovery operations and trusted brand;
6. documented, secure operations that can integrate without founder heroics.

Without at least several of these, the likely strategic choice is build, partner or ignore.

## Possible buyer archetypes

These are logical categories, not named prospects or indications of interest.

| Archetype | Potential rationale | What it will challenge |
|---|---|---|
| Consumer cyber/identity suite | Add household scam response, older-adult segment and family workflow | Why not extend its own AI/family product? |
| Credit/identity-data company | Add recurring protection and remediation engagement | Data rights, accuracy, claims and integration |
| Bank/credit-union technology vendor | Offer reusable member-protection benefit | Sponsor renewals, diligence readiness, financial-data boundary |
| Insurer/benefits platform | Engage caregivers or reduce relevant risk | Causal outcome proof and long sales cycles |
| Telecom/device/platform | Bundle safer communications and family support | Native capabilities, scale, latency and privacy |
| Financial-wellness or family-safety platform | Add trusted household workflow and cross-sell | Cohort retention, overlap and brand fit |

## Transaction evidence, with limits

Recent transactions show that cybersecurity, identity, recurring consumer relationships and distribution can have strategic value:

- Mastercard agreed to acquire threat-intelligence provider Recorded Future for $2.65 billion in 2024; the release cited 1,900 clients ([Mastercard](https://investor.mastercard.com/investor-news/investor-news-details/2024/Mastercard-Invests-in-Continued-Defense-of-Global-Digital-Economy-With-Acquisition-of-Recorded-Future/default.aspx)).
- Gen completed its roughly $1 billion MoneyLion acquisition in 2025 to expand financial wellness and customer engagement ([Gen](https://investor.gendigital.com/news/news-details/2025/Gen-Completes-Acquisition-of-MoneyLion-Accelerating-the-Companys-Leadership-in-Financial-Wellness/default.aspx)).
- TransUnion announced a $638 million Sontiq identity-protection acquisition in 2021 ([TransUnion presentation](https://investors.transunion.com/~/media/Files/T/Transunion-IR-V2/reports-and-presentations/agreement-to-acquire-sontiq.pdf)).
- Aura announced a proposed Qoria combination in 2026 and emphasized recurring revenue and family distribution; it remained subject to conditions ([Aura](https://www.aura.com/press/release/aura-qoria-acquisition-announcement)).

These companies, scale and assets are not comparable to BoomerBuddy. Their prices do not support a multiple, valuation or probability of exit here. They only suggest which asset qualities strategic buyers have discussed publicly.

## Readiness ledger from day one

**Corporate and IP**

- clean cap table, incorporation records, board/stock approvals and tax filings;
- invention and confidentiality assignments from every founder, employee and contractor;
- registered domains/marks, documented brand clearance and truthful marketing archive;
- open-source inventory/SBOM, license compliance and third-party asset/model rights.

**Data, privacy and safety**

- artifact-to-output lineage, consent purpose, retention/deletion and training-use records;
- dataset licenses, contributor rights, provenance and reproducible evaluation versions;
- segmented precision/recall, calibration, false-negative incidents and action-safety review;
- privacy notices, DPAs, subprocessors, data-transfer terms and request logs.

**Security and operations**

- access reviews, release/change controls, backups/restore tests, incident log and response exercises;
- architecture/data-flow records, vulnerability remediation and independent testing;
- uptime/support performance, business continuity and key-person succession.

**Commercial and financial**

- signed customer/partner contracts with pricing, data roles, reference rights, renewal, termination, assignment and change-of-control terms;
- monthly accrual financials reconciled to billing; revenue by customer/plan and deferred revenue;
- cohort retention, gross/contribution margin, CAC/payback and channel concentration;
- pipeline stages kept separate from contracts, launch and recognized revenue.

## Acquirer scorecard

Review quarterly:

- **Revenue:** MRR/ARR, growth, gross retention, expansion, cohort survival and quality of revenue.
- **Economics:** contribution margin and CAC payback by plan/channel; support and vendor sensitivity.
- **Distribution:** eligible → enrolled → activated sponsor funnel, sales cycle, renewal, concentration and reusable implementation.
- **Product:** first safe action, second use, Trusted Circle acceptance and collaborative action.
- **Safety:** representative evaluation coverage, calibration, harmful advice/false-negative review and incident closure.
- **Trust:** consent/revocation, deletion performance, accessibility, complaints and substantiated outcomes.
- **Independence:** release cadence, customer support and executive relationships operating without the founder.

No “loss prevented” total belongs in a data room unless methodology, evidence and counterfactual survive independent review.

## Milestones and kill conditions

**0–12 months:** establish clean ownership/rights, instrument cohorts/costs, produce a reproducible evaluation and win paid household evidence.  
**12–24 months:** demonstrate repeatable retention and contribution, at least one standardized sponsored renewal/expansion, low concentration and due-diligence-ready controls.  
**Later:** develop relationships across buyer archetypes through ordinary partnerships and product discussions; never represent those contacts as acquisition interest.

A $10 million-plus strategic-asset thesis becomes discussable only when a buyer can acquire scarce distribution, durable revenue, rights-cleared learning assets and operating capability faster and with less risk than building. There is no evidence that threshold has been met. Reassess or abandon the exit thesis if retention is incident-only, contracts are nonassignable, data rights are ambiguous, quality is not independently reproducible, one partner dominates, or the business depends on unverifiable claims or the founder’s personal presence.
