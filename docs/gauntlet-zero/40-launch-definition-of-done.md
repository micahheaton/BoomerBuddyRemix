# Launch Definition of Done

Build Run 1 is **not** a customer launch. Accepting the first dollar requires all items below.

## Product and evidence

- Older adults and adult-child pairs complete the critical loop in observed usability sessions.
- Name, positioning, pricing, and Family value have documented validation evidence.
- Text/URL evaluation meets agreed false-negative, false-positive, calibration, and action-safety thresholds on an independently reviewed set.
- Recovery guidance is reviewed against current authoritative sources.

## Security and privacy

- External application-security review has no open critical/high findings.
- Object-level authorization and cross-household/tenant regression tests pass.
- Production identity/MFA, secrets, encryption, backups/restore, rate limiting, abuse controls, retention, deletion/export, audit, incident response, and vendor DPAs are operational.
- Employee access and support escalation procedures are trained and tested.

## Commerce and legal

- Real provider webhooks are idempotent and reconciled; app-store entitlements are tested where sold.
- Renewal, cancellation, refund, grace, tax, and support workflows are complete.
- Qualified counsel has reviewed privacy, terms, disclaimers, auto-renewal, marketing/SMS consent, recording/audio, and partner requirements.

## Reliability and operations

- Staging mirrors production architecture; observability, alerts, runbooks, on-call ownership, restore and failure-mode tests pass.
- Accessibility audit plus assisted/manual older-adult testing has no critical blocker.
- Support scope, hours, severity, escalation, and incident ownership are truthful and staffed.

The launch owner signs a dated evidence checklist; a rendered UI or passing happy-path test cannot substitute.
