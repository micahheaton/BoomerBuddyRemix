# Credit Union Strategy

Status: **credible design-partner channel, not a proven sales channel**. No relationship, pilot or buyer intent is claimed. Research accessed 2026-08-15.

## Why this channel deserves a test

[NCUA Q1 2026 data](https://ncua.gov/newsroom/press-release/2026/ncua-releases-first-quarter-2026-credit-union-system-performance-data) reports 4,250 federally insured credit unions and 145.8 million memberships, including 748 “complex” institutions with more than $500 million in assets. Memberships are accounts across institutions, not unique people or BoomerBuddy TAM.

Financial institutions see relevant harm. FinCEN analyzed 155,415 Bank Secrecy Act filings describing about $27 billion in suspected elder financial exploitation from June 2022 through June 2023 ([FinCEN, April 2024](https://www.fincen.gov/news/news-releases/fincen-issues-analysis-elder-financial-exploitation)). That is suspicious activity reported by institutions, not adjudicated loss or revenue opportunity. The CFPB says banks and credit unions are uniquely positioned to detect and respond to exploitation ([CFPB](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/data-research/research-reports/reporting-suspected-elder-financial-exploitation-financial-institutions-update-2016-advisory-and-recommendations/)).

The countercase is strong: education may already be free, fraud teams prioritize transactions rather than family messages, sponsor adoption can be low, and [Carefull already markets directly to credit unions](https://getcarefull.com/credit-unions). BoomerBuddy must prove an incremental member outcome, not merely a sympathetic mission.

## Initial account profile

Use the current [NCUA active-research files](https://ncua.gov/analysis/credit-union-corporate-call-report-data) to build the universe; never infer age mix or purchase intent without evidence. Initial design-partner hypothesis:

- 10,000–250,000 members: enough scale to evaluate, potentially less procurement complexity than the largest systems;
- a named fraud/member-experience/digital executive and visible senior or caregiver education;
- leadership willingness to run a measured member-benefit evaluation;
- ability to launch through existing email, web, branch and contact-center channels;
- no requirement for core transaction data in the first phase.

Begin in a limited region only to make research and service feasible, not because public data proves regional demand. Score public facts, source every field, date it, and let humans review rankings.

## Buyer and value map

Likely champion: fraud prevention, member experience, digital banking, community impact or innovation leader. Economic buyer varies by institution. Information security, privacy, legal/compliance, procurement, marketing, contact center and executive sponsorship can all veto.

Test three buyer outcomes separately:

1. **Member value:** understandable second opinion and safe next action outside transaction monitoring.
2. **Family trust:** consented invitation of a trusted person without giving that person financial authority. CFPB’s [trusted-contact recommendations](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/documents/10240/cfpb_trusted-contacts-fis_2021-11.pdf) offer useful context, but BoomerBuddy’s Trusted Circle is not a legal or account-level trusted contact.
3. **Institutional value:** measurable adoption and a consistent education/escalation path, with less ad hoc support. Reduced fraud loss is an unproven later hypothesis.

## Low-integration paid evaluation

Run 90–120 days with a defined eligible cohort and one sponsor:

- co-branded eligibility page, orientation and enrollment;
- member-controlled accounts and sharing; no core credentials or transaction feed;
- a documented route to the credit union’s official contact details;
- aggregate sponsor reporting with small-cell suppression;
- member support and staff playbook;
- pre-agreed baseline, success measures, security terms and renewal decision date.

Primary measures: eligible → enrolled → oriented → first check → safe action → second use/Trusted Circle activation; comprehension, support minutes, complaint/opt-out rate and 30/60/90-day survival. A warning, click or self-reported hypothetical loss is not “loss prevented.” Any case study needs the institution’s written approval and a defensible methodology.

Pricing structures in [15-commercial-model-pricing.md](15-commercial-model-pricing.md) are interview hypotheses only. A pilot should be paid, tightly scoped and creditable to a production term; no bespoke core integration or permanent free proof of concept.

## Procurement readiness

NCUA guidance expects credit unions to plan, perform due diligence, negotiate controls and monitor third-party relationships ([NCUA third-party guidance](https://ncua.gov/regulation-supervision/letters-credit-unions-other-guidance/evaluating-third-party-relationships)). Prepare:

- security/data-flow architecture, retention/deletion, encryption and access controls;
- privacy notice, DPA, subprocessors and model-provider data terms;
- incident response, business continuity/disaster recovery, service levels and insurance;
- independent testing roadmap, vulnerability management and software inventory;
- accessibility conformance evidence and member-support process;
- financial condition, references when real, contract assignment/termination and audit rights.

NCUA explicitly does not approve vendors or label them “NCUA compliant” ([NCUA](https://ncua.gov/about/doing-business-credit-unions)). Federally insured credit unions have a 72-hour NCUA notification rule for certain cyber incidents, including some third-party notices ([NCUA](https://ncua.gov/regulation-supervision/letters-credit-unions-other-guidance/cyber-incident-notification-requirements)); contracts should support the institution’s timing, without claiming the rule directly certifies BoomerBuddy.

## Validation gates

Complete ten discovery interviews before selecting accounts. Proceed to proposals only when at least three buyers independently confirm an owner, budget path, acceptable first-phase data scope and renewal metric. Seek two paid design partners with the same reusable offer. Scale only after one renews/expands, activation is meaningful, security burden is supportable and sponsor-channel contribution clears [36-unit-economics.md](36-unit-economics.md). Stop if buyers demand unprovable loss guarantees, transaction access before value, white-label custom builds or unpaid indefinite pilots.
